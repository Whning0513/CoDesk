# Lamp shade geometry review

A adjusts the Blender scene's Lamp_Shade and publishes the saved project. B reviews that saved project in Blender and records its measured shade width, bulb radial clearance and verdict in shade_review.txt. The bulb is 0.50 m wide. Radial clearance is half the difference between shade and bulb widths; at least 0.40 m is required. Report the actual saved dimensions even if they disagree with a message. Preserve all other objects.
